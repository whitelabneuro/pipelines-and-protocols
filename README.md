# WhiteLabNeuro: Pipelines & Protocols

**White Lab Neuro** is the GitHub home for selected pipelines, workflows and version-controlled protocols developed in White Lab at King’s College London.

Our research focuses on **RNA-binding proteins, human stem-cell models, transcriptomics and brain disease**, with an emphasis on transparent and reproducible approaches to experimental and computational work.

This repository is intended to grow alongside the lab and to provide a clear, structured record of how methods and analyses are organised across projects.

---

## What this repository contains

This repository is being developed as a central home for:

- **Computational pipelines** used for transcriptomic and related analyses
- **Version-controlled protocols** for experimental and analytical workflows
- **Project-linked resources** associated with datasets, figures or manuscripts where appropriate
- **Documentation** to support consistency, traceability and reuse

As the lab grows, this repository will increasingly support internal organisation, collaboration and open science.
